🔑 2. Practice Fill-in-the-Blank Drill

Complete each sentence with the most appropriate collocation from the bank above.

1. Without governmental oversight, machine-learning algorithms will continue to perpetuate .................................... in judicial sentencing.

2. The rapid spread of international media outlets threatens to .................................... indigenous traditions and dialects.

3. Municipal governments must allocate resources toward .................................... such as smoking cessation and nutritional education.

4. Heavy subsidies for solar panel installations will empower factories to .................................... more rapidly.

5. In modern economies, a university degree in engineering is considered a powerful .................................... .

🔑 3. Answer Key & Rationales

1. algorithmic bias (Context: Machine learning in judicial sentencing).

2. homogenize local cultures (Context: International media replacing indigenous traditions).

3. preventative healthcare measures (Context: Nutritional education and smoking cessation).

4. transition to renewables / renewable energy sources (Context: Solar panel installation subsidies).

5. catalyst for economic growth / socio-economic mobility (Context: University degree facilitating career and economic advancement).
