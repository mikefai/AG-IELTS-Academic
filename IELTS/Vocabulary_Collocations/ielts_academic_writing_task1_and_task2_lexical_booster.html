✍️ IELTS Academic Writing: Task 1 & Task 2 Lexical Booster (Band 7.0 ➔ 9.0)

📋 Module Overview

  • Exam Focus: Lexical Resource (LR) for Academic Writing Task 1 & Task 2
  • Target Transition: Band 7.0 ➔ 9.0
  • The Core Problem for Band 6.0–6.5 Candidates:

- In Task 1: Repeating "increased/decreased" 10 times and copying raw percentages ("25%, 50%, 75%") without lexical proportion rephrasing ("a quarter, the vast majority, three-quarters").

- In Task 2: Relying on generic, low-register verbs ("shows", "makes", "helps", "thinks") instead of analytical academic verbs ("substantiates", "precipitates", "underpins", "exacerbates").

  • Pedagogical Objectives:

1. Master Dynamic Trend & Degree Adverbials for Task 1 data movements.

2. Implement the Proportion & Fraction Rephrasing System to eliminate raw number repetition.

3. Deploy High-Precision Academic Argumentative Verbs & Discourse Markers for Task 2 essays.

👨‍🏫 1. Comprehensive Teacher Lesson Plan & Classroom Guide

Pacing & Lesson Flow (60-Minute Model)

Stage Timing Pedagogical Action & Classroom Task Interaction
1. The "Repetition Trap" Warmer 8 min Present a student Task 1 paragraph with 6 instances of "increased" and "25%". Ask students: "How does lexical repetition cap Lexical Resource at Band 6.0?" Plenary
2. Trend & Fraction Matrix 12 min Teach the 4 categories of data variation: Trajectory, Rate/Magnitude, Proportion Synonyms, and Data Plateauing. Teacher-led
3. Task 2 Analytical Verb Lab 10 min Contrast low-level verbs with their academic equivalents ("makes worse" ➔ "exacerbates", "supports" ➔ "corroborates"). Plenary
4. 3-Stage Skill Building 15 min Pairs complete Exercise 1 (Fraction & Proportion Transformer) and Exercise 2 (Analytical Verb Nuance Sifter). Pairs ➔ Plenary
5. Applied Writing Arena 15 min Students write a 100-word comparative data report using at least 4 proportion rephrasings and 3 upgraded verbs in the interactive Writing Arena. Individual

Concept Checking Questions (CCQs) for Teachers

  • Q: "Can I write 'a half of the people' in Task 1?"

A: No. Write "half the population" or "50 percent of respondents" (drop the "a" before "half").

  • Q: "What is the difference between 'plateaued' and 'stabilized'?"

A: Plateaued describes leveling off after an upward climb; stabilized describes settling into an even trend after fluctuations.

🔍 3. Guided Exercise: Annotated Lexical Transformation Lab

Original Band 5.5 Data Description (Repetitive & Mechanical):

"In 2010, 50% of people used cars. In 2015, this number increased to 75%. In 2020, it decreased to 25%. Solar energy was 2% in 2010 and it increased to 10% in 2020. This shows that solar energy is becoming popular."

Upgraded Band 8.5 Model (Lexical Variety & Proportions Integrated):

"In 2010, exactly half the surveyed population commuted via private automobiles. Over the subsequent five-year period, this figure surged dramatically to peak at three-quarters of the cohort, before plummeting precipitously to a mere quarter by 2020. Conversely, solar energy adoption, which represented a negligible 2% share initially, registered a fivefold expansion to capture one in ten households, substantiating a pronounced transition towards renewable energy consumption."

Key Upgrades Analyzed:

1. "50% of people" ➔ "exactly half the surveyed population".

2. "increased to 75%" ➔ "surged dramatically to peak at three-quarters of the cohort".

3. "decreased to 25%" ➔ "plummeting precipitously to a mere quarter".

4. "2% in 2010" ➔ "a negligible 2% share initially".

5. "increased to 10%" ➔ "registered a fivefold expansion to capture one in ten households".

6. "This shows that" ➔ "substantiating a pronounced transition towards".

🔑 5. Complete Answer Key & Step-by-Step Rationales

Exercise 1 Answer Key

1. Model: "Approximately one in three tertiary graduates [or just under a third of the graduate cohort] struggled to secure employment."

2. Model: "The vast majority of households, representing nearly three-quarters of the total, possessed two or more motor vehicles."

3. Model: "Electric vehicles accounted for a negligible minority of just 3% of overall automotive purchases in 2015."

Exercise 2 Answer Key

1. exacerbates (formal academic verb meaning to make an already bad situation worse).

2. substantiate (precise academic verb meaning to provide evidence supporting a claim).

3. erodes (metaphorical academic verb describing the gradual destruction of value/power).

4. precipitates (high-register verb meaning to cause an undesirable event to happen suddenly).

Exercise 3 Model Answer

"Between 2010 and 2020, City X underwent a radical restructuring of its energy profile. Coal power, which initially constituted nearly two-thirds (65%) of the total energy supply, plummeted precipitously to represent a mere one-fifth (20%) by the end of the decade. Conversely, wind energy registered a dramatic fourfold expansion, escalating from one in ten households (10%) to account for fully two-fifths (40%) of municipal generation. Throughout this period, nuclear power generation remained remarkably stable, consistently supplying precisely one-quarter (25%) of the city's electricity demand."
