# 🎯 IELTS Academic: Band 9.0 Lexical Resource Master Matrix

## 📌 Executive Overview
Scoring **Band 8.5–9.0 in Lexical Resource (LR)** requires using sophisticated, natural, and flexible academic collocations, precise topical terminology, and idiomatic discourse markers with zero unnatural force-fitting. This master matrix organizes high-scoring Tier 3 academic vocabulary across the **8 most frequent IELTS Academic writing and speaking topics**.

## 🧠 Band 9 Examiner Scoring Checklist for Lexical Resource

- [ ] **Precision**: Do your word choices convey exact, nuanced meaning rather than vague generalities? (*e.g., "anthropogenic greenhouse emissions" vs "human pollution"*).
- [ ] **Collocational Accuracy**: Are adjectives, verbs, and prepositions naturally bonded without awkward phrasing? (*e.g., "curtail emissions", "foster critical thinking"*).
- [ ] **Academic Register**: Is the vocabulary formal and objective, avoiding slang, colloquialisms, and conversational shortcuts (*kids $\rightarrow$ children/adolescents, bad $\rightarrow$ detrimental/adverse*)?
- [ ] **Variety**: Do you avoid repeating the same head nouns and verbs throughout your essay by deploying diverse syntactic transformations?
