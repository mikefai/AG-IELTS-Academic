📚 IELTS Academic Vocabulary: Top 100 Collocations & Lexical Resource Masterclass (Band 7.0 ➔ 9.0)

📋 Module Overview

  • Exam Focus: Lexical Resource for Writing (Task 1 & Task 2) and Speaking (Parts 1, 2, 3)
  • Target Transition: Band 7.0 ➔ 9.0
  • The Core Problem for Band 6.0–6.5 Candidates:

- Memorizing isolated, single words out of context (e.g. "ubiquitous", "plethora") and using them unnaturally (awkward phrasing / incorrect preposition pairings).

- Translating idioms word-for-word from L1 instead of using authentic academic verb-noun and adjective-noun collocations.

  • Pedagogical Objectives:

1. Master 100 High-Frequency Academic Collocations grouped across 5 recurring IELTS thematic pillars.

2. Eliminate Prepositional & Collocational Mismatches ("make research" ❌ ➔ "conduct research" ✅).

3. Deploy Natural Phrasal Verbs and High-Register Nominalizations.

👨‍🏫 1. Comprehensive Teacher Lesson Plan & Classroom Guide

Pacing & Lesson Flow (60-Minute Model)

Stage Timing Pedagogical Action & Classroom Task Interaction
1. Diagnostic Collocation Hook 8 min Display 5 classic candidate errors on the board ("make an experiment", "raise the economic", "play a role into"). Ask students to identify why grammatical sentences can still sound un-idiomatic. Plenary
2. Thematic Collocation Matrix 12 min Introduce the 5 Thematic Pillars (Environment, Technology, Education/Work, Urban Society, Economics). Teach adjective-noun and verb-noun pairing rules. Teacher-led
3. Guided Substitution Lab 10 min Walk through the Guided Lexical Transformation text, converting a Band 5.5 paragraph into a Band 8.5 academic text live. Plenary
4. 3-Stage Skill Building 15 min Pairs complete Exercise 1 (Collocation Matcher) and Exercise 2 (The False Friend & Error Gym). Pairs ➔ Plenary
5. Applied Writing/Speaking Arena 15 min Students write 3 thematic sentences incorporating 6 target collocations and test them with the Audio Reader. Individual

Concept Checking Questions (CCQs) for Teachers

  • Q: "Why is 'do research' or 'conduct research' correct, but 'make research' incorrect?"

A: Collocation rules. Certain verbs naturally co-occur with specific nouns in English. Using incorrect verbs signals non-native L1 translation.

  • Q: "Can I use informal idioms like 'raining cats and dogs' or 'a piece of cake' in Academic Writing?"

A: No. Academic Writing Task 1 & 2 requires formal lexical collocations ("exerts a profound influence", "mitigates environmental risks"), not colloquial idioms.

🔍 3. Guided Exercise: Annotated Lexical Transformation Lab

Original Band 5.5 Paragraph (Weak Lexicon & Repetitive Verbs):

"Big cities have many problems with traffic. People buy more cars and this makes a lot of pollution that is bad for nature. The government needs to do something fast to stop this problem and help people use buses more so they can have better lives."

Upgraded Band 8.5 Model (High-Yield Collocations Integrated):

"Modern metropolitan centers face escalating logistical challenges stemming from vehicular congestion. The rapid expansion of private automobile ownership exerts severe pressure on municipal infrastructure and precipitates catastrophic environmental degradation. To mitigate these adverse effects, municipal authorities must implement stringent regulatory frameworks while expanding subsidized mass transit networks, thereby fostering sustainable urban mobility and safeguarding public health."

Line-by-Line Collocation Annotations:

1. "face escalating logistical challenges" ➔ Replaces "have many problems".

2. "exerts severe pressure on municipal infrastructure" ➔ Replaces "makes traffic worse".

3. "precipitates catastrophic environmental degradation" ➔ Replaces "makes a lot of pollution that is bad".

4. "mitigate these adverse effects" ➔ Replaces "stop this problem".

5. "implement stringent regulatory frameworks" ➔ Replaces "do something fast".

6. "subsidized mass transit networks" ➔ Replaces "help people use buses".

7. "fostering sustainable urban mobility" ➔ Replaces "have better lives".

🔑 5. Complete Answer Key & Step-by-Step Rationales

Exercise 1 Answer Key

  • 1 ➔ B (mitigate the adverse environmental consequences)
  • 2 ➔ C (cultivate critical analytical skills)
  • 3 ➔ A (bridge the digital divide)
  • 4 ➔ E (spearhead innovative scientific research)
  • 5 ➔ D (enforce stringent emission caps)

Exercise 2 Answer Key

1. Correction: "Scientists are planning to conduct [or undertake / carry out] comprehensive research into renewable biofuels."

Rationale: English collocations pair conduct/undertake/carry out with research, never make.

2. Correction: "The rapid growth of the city has inflicted severe damage on [or wrought havoc upon] the local forest."

Rationale: Replace played a big damage with inflicted severe damage.

3. Correction: "Governments must enact [or pass / implement] strict legislation against illegal deforestation."

Rationale: Laws are enacted, passed, or implemented, not taken.

4. Correction: "This policy will yield [or deliver] significant economic dividends for the country."

Rationale: Dividends are yielded or delivered, not given.

Exercise 3 Answer Key

1. Band 8.5 Upgrade: "Chronically underfunded educational institutions frequently lack modern technological infrastructure, thereby failing to bridge the digital divide for underprivileged students."

2. Band 8.5 Upgrade: "The habitual consumption of ultra-processed food paired with sedentary modern lifestyles serves to escalate public health burdens across urban populations."

3. Band 8.5 Upgrade: "Uncontrolled deforestation precipitates irreversible biodiversity loss, permanently disrupting the region's fragile ecological balance."
