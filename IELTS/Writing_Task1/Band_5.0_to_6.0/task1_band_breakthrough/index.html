🏗️ 3. The 4-Paragraph Skeleton — Universal Essay Blueprint

Follow this strict formula for every Task 1 report

Paragraph 1: The Introduction

1–2 Sentences • ~30 Words

Purpose: Paraphrase the question prompt stimulus. Never copy more than 3 words verbatim from the prompt, as copied words are deducted from your total word count.

❌ Band 5 Copied Prompt:
"The graph below shows the amount of electricity produced in three countries between 2000 and 2020."
✅ Band 7+ Paraphrase:
"The line graph illustrates power generation across three nations over a two-decade period from 2000 to 2020."

Paragraph 2: The Overview ⭐ (The Paragraph That Decides Your Band)

2–3 Sentences • ~40 Words

Purpose: Summarize the 2–3 most striking macro trends or main features. Always start with "Overall, ...". NEVER include specific figures, numbers, or percentages here! Save all numbers for paragraphs 3 and 4.

✅ Band 7+ Overview Template:
"Overall, power generation increased significantly in all three countries over the period. In addition, Country A consistently generated the largest quantity of electricity, whereas Country C remained the lowest producer throughout."

Paragraph 3: Detail Paragraph 1 (First Logical Group)

3–4 Sentences • ~50 Words

Purpose: Describe the first logical group of data (e.g. all increasing trends, or the top 2 highest categories). Support all statements with accurate figures, starting points, and peak numbers.

✅ Example Strategy: Group Country A and Country B together because both experienced steady upward trajectories, highlighting specific dates and kilowatt figures.

Paragraph 4: Detail Paragraph 2 (Second Logical Group & Comparisons)

3–4 Sentences • ~50 Words

Purpose: Describe the remaining data (e.g. declining categories, anomalies, or minor sectors). Use strong comparative transition markers ("In stark contrast", "Conversely", "Meanwhile"). No conclusion paragraph is required!

✅ Example Strategy: Focus on Country C and make direct comparative contrasts with Countries A and B using figures to validate the gap.
