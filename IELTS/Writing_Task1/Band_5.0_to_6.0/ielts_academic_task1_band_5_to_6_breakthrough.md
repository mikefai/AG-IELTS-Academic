# 🎯 IELTS Academic Writing Task 1: Band 6+ Breakthrough Masterclass

## 📋 Module Overview
- **Domain**: IELTS Academic Writing Task 1
- **Target Audience**: Candidates scoring Band 5.0–5.5 who struggle with mechanical data listing, missing overviews, and lexical repetition.
- **Goal**: Achieve a consistent Band 6.5–7.5 score by executing the 4-Paragraph Skeleton, writing high-impact macro-overviews with zero numbers, and utilizing functional academic collocations.
- **Interactive Companion Webapp**: Available in [`task1_band_breakthrough/index.html`](task1_band_breakthrough/index.html).

## 🎯 1. Know the Battlefield — Exam Structure & 4 Criteria

| Parameter | Specification | Examiner Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recommended Time** | 20 Minutes | Spend no more than 20 mins to leave 40 mins for Task 2 (worth 2/3 of total score). |
| **Minimum Word Count** | 150 Words | **Writing under 150 words caps Task Achievement at Band 5.** Target: 160–190 words. |
| **Scoring Weight** | 33.3% (1/3 of Writing) | Divided into 4 equally weighted criteria (25% each). |

### The 4 Criteria Breakdown: Band 6 vs Band 7

```
                  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │              4 CRITERIA OF TASK 1 (25% EACH)           │
                  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                       │                  │                  │                  │
         ┌─────────────┴──────┐ ┌────────┴─────────┐ ┌───────┴────────┐ ┌──────┴──────────────┐
         │  Task Achievement  │ │  Coherence &     │ │    Lexical     │ │  Grammatical Range   │
         │       (TA)         │ │  Cohesion (CC)   │ │  Resource (LR) │ │  & Accuracy (GRA)    │
         └────────────────────┘ └──────────────────┘ └────────────────┘ └─────────────────────┘
```

1. **Task Achievement (TA)**:
   - *Band 6 Requirement*: Presents an overview with some information. Highlights main trends, but some data may be mechanical or missing key details.
   - *Band 7 Requirement*: Provides a clear, well-developed overview. Key features are clearly identified, highlighted, and logically illustrated with accurate data.
   - 🚨 **The Hard Rule**: *A missing or unclear overview (or putting numbers in the overview) limits Task Achievement to Band 5.*

2. **Coherence & Cohesion (CC)**:
   - *Band 6 Requirement*: Organizes information coherently with clear overall progression. Uses cohesive devices, but some may be repetitive or mechanical.
   - *Band 7 Requirement*: Information and ideas are logically sequenced with clear progression throughout. Uses a varied range of cohesive devices seamlessly.

3. **Lexical Resource (LR)**:
   - *Band 6 Requirement*: Adequate range of vocabulary. Attempts less common vocabulary with some errors in word choice, spelling, or collocation.
   - *Band 7 Requirement*: Uses a sufficient range of vocabulary with flexibility and precision. Accurately uses less common collocations with rare minor slips.

4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA)**:
   - *Band 6 Requirement*: Uses a mix of simple and complex sentence forms. Makes some grammatical and punctuation errors, though meaning remains clear.
   - *Band 7 Requirement*: Uses a variety of complex structures (passives, relative clauses, adverbials). Produces frequent error-free sentences.

## 📊 2. The 6 Question Types

1. **Line Graph**: Continuous time trends. Focus on starting/ending points, overall trajectories, and peak/intersection points.
2. **Bar Chart**: Discrete comparisons. Focus on tallest/shortest bars and category rank order.
3. **Pie Chart**: Proportions totaling 100%. Focus on dominant slices and percentage shifts.
4. **Table**: Dense data. Focus on row/column totals, highs/lows, and overarching patterns.
5. **Process Diagram**: Chronological transformation. Focus on step count, raw inputs, transformation stages, and final outputs (Use Passive Voice!).
6. **Map / Floor Plan**: Spatial/urban development over time. Focus on additions, demolitions, expansions, and directional prepositions.

## 🏗️ 3. The 4-Paragraph Skeleton

- **Paragraph 1: Introduction (~30 words, 1–2 sentences)**: Paraphrase the prompt stimulus. Never copy >3 words verbatim.
- **Paragraph 2: The Overview (~40 words, 2–3 sentences)**: Identify 2–3 macro trends. Start with *"Overall, ..."*. **NEVER include numbers, percentages, or dates!**
- **Paragraph 3: Detail Paragraph 1 (~50 words, 3–4 sentences)**: Group the first logical subset of data (e.g. upward trends). Provide exact numbers and starting/ending values.
- **Paragraph 4: Detail Paragraph 2 (~50 words, 3–4 sentences)**: Group the remaining data (e.g. downward trends or anomalies). Contrast with Paragraph 3. *(No conclusion needed).*

## 💀 4. The 7 Deadly Sins of Task 1

1. **Listing Every Single Number**: Turns your essay into an unreadable spreadsheet.
2. **Missing or Number-Heavy Overview**: Triggers a hard Band 5 cap in Task Achievement.
3. **Inserting Personal Opinions**: You are an objective reporter, not an essayist.
4. **Copying the Prompt Word-for-Word**: Copied words are deducted from your word count.
5. **Wrong Tense for Time Period**: Past dates require Past Simple; future projections require *"is projected to"*.
6. **Repeating "Shows" and "Increased"**: Causes vocabulary stagnation at Band 5.
7. **Wall of Text (No Paragraphing)**: Severe penalty in Coherence & Cohesion.

## 🔑 5. Complete Model Response (Band 8.0 Level)

### Prompt
> The line graph illustrates the percentage of households with internet access in three European countries (UK, Germany, Spain) between 2000 and 2020.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    BAND 8.0 ESSAY                                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [P1: Intro]                                                                           |
| The line graph illustrates the proportion of households with access to the            |
| internet across three European nations—the UK, Germany, and Spain—over a              |
| 20-year period from 2000 to 2020.                                                     |
|                                                                                       |
| [P2: Overview]                                                                        |
| Overall, all three countries experienced significant upward trajectories in home      |
| internet connectivity over the two decades. Notably, while the UK consistently        |
| maintained the highest penetration rates throughout, Spain recorded the most rapid    |
| acceleration despite starting from the lowest base.                                   |
|                                                                                       |
| [P3: Detail 1]                                                                        |
| Looking first at the leading nations, the UK began the period with roughly 30% of    |
| households connected in 2000. This figure climbed steadily to surpass the 50% mark    |
| by 2010, before culminating at a peak of precisely 90% by 2020. Similarly, Germany    |
| followed a parallel trajectory, rising from 20% in 2000 to approximately 80% at the   |
| end of the period, trailing the UK by roughly 10 percentage points throughout.        |
|                                                                                       |
| [P4: Detail 2]                                                                        |
| In stark contrast, Spain initially registered a modest adoption rate of merely 10%    |
| in 2000. However, after moderate growth to 35% in 2010, Spanish internet connectivity  |
| surged dramatically over the final decade, reaching 70% in 2020 and substantially    |
| narrowing the gap with its northern counterparts.                                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 🔑 6. Classroom Drill & Answer Key

### Drill: Paraphrase the Following Prompt
> **Original**: "The bar chart below shows the number of cars produced in Japan, the USA, and Germany from 2005 to 2015."

- **Model Paraphrase**: *"The bar chart compares automotive manufacturing output across Japan, the United States, and Germany over a ten-year span from 2005 to 2015."*
- **Rationale**:
  - *"shows"* ➔ *"compares / illustrates"*
  - *"the number of cars produced"* ➔ *"automotive manufacturing output"*
  - *"in Japan, the USA, and Germany"* ➔ *"across Japan, the United States, and Germany"*
  - *"from 2005 to 2015"* ➔ *"over a ten-year span from 2005 to 2015"*
