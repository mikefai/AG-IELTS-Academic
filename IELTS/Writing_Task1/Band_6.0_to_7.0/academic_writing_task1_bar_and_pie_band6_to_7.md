# ✍️ IELTS Academic Writing Task 1: Bar & Pie Charts (Band 6.0 ➔ 7.0)

## 📋 Module Overview
- **Skill Focus**: Academic Writing Task 1 (Bar Charts & Pie Charts / Proportions)
- **Target Transition**: Band 6.0 ➔ 7.0
- **Key Focus**: Grouping multi-variable discrete data, avoiding bar-by-bar listing, mastering proportion fractions and comparative syntax.

---

## 🎯 1. Crucial Differences Between Band 6 and Band 7

| Assessment Criterion | Band 6 Performance | Band 7 Target Performance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task Achievement (TA)** | Describes data bar-by-bar; overview mentions a broad observation but may include numbers or miss key secondary contrasts. | Synthesizes categories into 2 macro-trends in the overview; details are grouped logically into 2 paragraphs with clear comparative links. |
| **Coherence & Cohesion (CC)** | Uses basic connectors repetitively (*"Also", "In addition", "And then"*). | Uses varied cohesive markers (*"In stark contrast", "Ranked second", "Followed closely by", "Whereas"*). |
| **Lexical Resource (LR)** | Relies on *"was higher than"*, *"had more percent"*, *"was 40%"*. | Uses sophisticated proportion vocabulary (*"accounted for the lion's share", "a negligible fraction", "comprised nearly a third"*). |
| **Grammatical Range (GRA)** | Simple sentences (*"Item A was 50%. Item B was 30%."*). | Complex comparative clauses (*"While Item A constituted half the total, Item B accounted for merely a fraction at 15%."*). |

## 📊 2. Proportion & Fraction Lexicon Reference

```
+---------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| PERCENTAGE    | FRACTIONAL / RATIO EQUIVALENT   | ACADEMIC PHRASAL UPGRADE                    |
+---------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| 75% – 80%     | Three-quarters / Four-fifths    | "Constituted the vast majority of..."       |
| 50% – 52%     | Precisely half / Just over half | "Accounted for exactly half the total..."   |
| 33% – 35%     | Roughly one-third               | "Represented slightly more than a third..." |
| 25%           | Exactly a quarter / one-fourth  | "Made up precisely one-fourth of..."        |
| 10% – 12%     | Approximately one-tenth         | "Recorded a modest share of one in ten..."  |
| 2% – 4%       | A negligible slice / fraction   | "Formed a minuscule proportion of..."       |
+---------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
```

## 📝 3. Practice Prompt & Full Band 7.5 Model Response

### Prompt
> **The bar chart and pie chart show the main sources of energy used in Australia in 2010 and the projected distribution of energy consumption by sector for 2030.**  
> *Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

```
=========================================================================================
                                   BAND 7.5 MODEL ESSAY
=========================================================================================
[Paragraph 1: Introduction]
The provided charts illustrate the primary energy sources utilized across Australia in 2010, 
alongside forecasted sectoral energy consumption proportions for the year 2030.

[Paragraph 2: The Overview]
Overall, fossil fuels—predominantly coal and natural gas—constituted the overwhelming 
majority of Australia's energy mix in 2010, while renewable sources represented a negligible 
share. Looking ahead to 2030, industrial manufacturing and commercial transportation are 
projected to remain the two dominant consumers of national energy output.

[Paragraph 3: Body 1 — Energy Sources in 2010]
In 2010, coal was by far the leading energy source, accounting for nearly half (48%) of all 
energy generated. Natural gas followed as the second largest contributor at 24%, while crude 
oil supplied roughly a fifth (20%) of total production. In stark contrast, renewable options 
played a minimal role; hydroelectric power accounted for merely 5%, and combined solar and 
wind energy represented a minuscule fraction at just 3%.

[Paragraph 4: Body 2 — Sectoral Projections for 2030]
Turning to consumption forecasts for 2030, the industrial sector is expected to consume the 
largest proportion of national energy at 42%. Transport follows closely behind, representing 
almost a third (31%) of projected demand. Conversely, residential and agricultural uses are 
forecasted to require significantly smaller shares, comprising 16% and 11% respectively.
=========================================================================================
```

## 🔍 4. Step-by-Step Examiner Commentary

1. **Task Achievement (Band 8.0)**:
   - The overview clearly identifies the macro-pattern of both graphics without citing any numbers.
   - All main energy sources and sectors are reported and grouped systematically.
2. **Coherence & Cohesion (Band 7.5)**:
   - Paragraph 3 handles energy sources (chart 1); Paragraph 4 handles sectoral demand (chart 2).
   - Cohesive transitions (*"Turning to", "In stark contrast", "Conversely", "followed closely behind"*) ensure smooth flow.
3. **Lexical Resource (Band 7.5)**:
   - Variety of proportion synonyms: *overwhelming majority, negligible share, leading energy source, crude oil supplied roughly a fifth, minuscule fraction*.
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Band 7.5)**:
   - Accurate control of future projection structures: *is projected to remain, is expected to consume, are forecasted to require*.
