# ✍️ IELTS Academic Writing Task 1: Process & Flowchart Masterclass

## 📋 Module Overview
- **Skill Focus**: Academic Writing Task 1 (Process Diagrams, Industrial Manufacturing, Natural Cycles)
- **Target Transition**: Band 7.0 ➔ 8.0
- **Key Challenges**: Present Simple Passive voice consistency, chronological sequencing without repeating *"then/next"*, summarizing stage counts in the overview.

---

## 🎯 1. Master Formula for Process Diagrams

### The 3 Core Rules
1. **Overview Formula**:
   - Total number of discrete stages from start to finish.
   - Initial raw input material ➔ Final usable product or natural phase.
   - *Example*: *"Overall, the process comprises eight distinct sequential stages, beginning with the mechanical harvesting of raw sugarcane and culminating in the packaging of refined white sugar crystals for commercial distribution."*
2. **Passive Voice Domination**:
   - Because the human actor is irrelevant, 80%+ of verbs in an industrial process must be in the **Present Simple Passive** (`is/are + Past Participle`):
     - *Active (Band 5)*: "The workers crush the sugar cane." ❌
     - *Passive (Band 8)*: "The sugarcane **is crushed** using heavy steel rollers." ✅
3. **Advanced Sequential Adverbials**:
   - Replace *"then / after that"* with:
     - *"Subsequent to this..."*
     - *"Having been filtered, the liquid is..."* (Participle clause)
     - *"Prior to entering the drying kiln..."*
     - *"Simultaneously, the secondary residue is diverted to..."*

## 🏭 2. Process Diagram Prompt & Model Response

### Prompt
> **The diagram illustrates the stages involved in the commercial production of roasted Arabica coffee beans for retail sale.**  
> *Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

```
=========================================================================================
                                   BAND 8.0 MODEL ESSAY
=========================================================================================
[Paragraph 1: Introduction]
The provided diagram delineates the multi-stage industrial procedure involved in the 
commercial processing and roasting of Arabica coffee cherries into packaged coffee beans.

[Paragraph 2: The Overview]
Overall, the process encompasses seven distinct operational phases, initiating with the 
manual harvesting of ripe coffee berries, progressing through wet processing and roasting, 
and culminating in the vacuum-sealing of finished roasted beans for retail distribution.

[Paragraph 3: Body 1 — Harvesting to Drying]
In the initial stage, red coffee cherries are handpicked from plantations to ensure optimal 
ripeness. Following collection, the cherries undergo wet processing, where they are 
immersed in water tanks to remove floating debris before being mechanically depulped to 
separate the outer fruit flesh from the internal seeds. The extracted beans are subsequently 
transferred to fermentation vats for 24 to 48 hours to dissolve residual mucilage, after 
which they are rinsed with clean water and spread evenly across elevated drying beds to 
dehydrate under natural sunlight until moisture levels drop below 12%.

[Paragraph 4: Body 2 — Hulling, Roasting & Packaging]
Once fully dried, the parchment-covered beans undergo mechanical hulling to strip away the 
outer husk, yielding raw green coffee beans. These sorted beans are then introduced into 
industrial rotating drum roasters operating at approximately 200°C to 220°C, where intense 
thermal processing induces the Maillard reaction, transforming the green beans into aromatic, 
dark-brown roasted beans. Following rapid cooling with cold air streams to halt further 
cooking, the finalized beans are immediately packaged into airtight vacuum bags equipped 
with one-way degassing valves to preserve freshness.
=========================================================================================
```

## 🔍 3. Examiner Rubric Analysis

1. **Task Achievement (Band 8.5)**:
   - Identifies total stage count (7 phases) and endpoints in the overview.
   - Covers all technical steps without omitting intermediate phases or inserting extraneous external explanations.
2. **Coherence & Cohesion (Band 8.5)**:
   - Sophisticated sequencing: *In the initial stage, Following collection, are subsequently transferred, Once fully dried, Following rapid cooling, immediately packaged*.
3. **Lexical Resource (Band 8.0)**:
   - High-precision technical lexicon: *mechanically depulped, fermentation vats, residual mucilage, elevated drying beds, mechanical hulling, rotating drum roasters, vacuum-sealed*.
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Band 8.5)**:
   - Flawless passive voice integration paired with complex participial clauses (*"yielding raw green coffee beans", "equipped with one-way degassing valves"*).
