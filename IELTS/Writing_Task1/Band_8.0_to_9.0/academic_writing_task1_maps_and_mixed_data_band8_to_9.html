✍️ IELTS Academic Writing Task 1: Urban Maps & Mixed Graphics (Band 9.0)

📋 Module Overview

  • Skill Focus: Academic Writing Task 1 (Urban Redevelopment Maps & Multiple Mixed Visuals)
  • Target Level: Band 8.5 ➔ 9.0
  • Key Challenges: Synthesizing spatial modifications across time periods, avoiding localized list-like descriptions, integrating compass orientations, handling mixed charts (e.g. Line + Bar combination).

🎯 1. The Spatial Transformation Lexicon for Band 9.0

Code
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------+
| CATEGORY OF URBAN CHANGE          | ACADEMIC PHRASAL / VERBAL UPGRADES                        |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------+
| Demolition / Removal              | "was demolished to make way for...", "was flattened"      |
| Construction / Addition           | "was erected", "sprang up on the former site of..."       |
| Repurposing / Conversion          | "was repurposed into...", "underwent commercial conversion"|
| Expansion / Enlargement           | "expanded substantially eastward", "doubled in footprint"  |
| Infrastructure & Transit          | "was integrated with a dual-carriageway bypass..."        |
| Preservation / No Change          | "remained virtually unaltered throughout the period"      |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------+

🗺️ 2. Map Prompt & Full Band 9.0 Model Response

Prompt

The two maps illustrate the coastal settlement of Port Henderson in 1995 and following extensive commercial redevelopment in 2020. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
Code
=========================================================================================
                                    BAND 9.0 MODEL ESSAY
=========================================================================================
[Paragraph 1: Introduction]
The maps depict the extensive infrastructural and commercial modernization undertaken in the 
coastal settlement of Port Henderson over a 25-year timeframe from 1995 to 2020.

[Paragraph 2: The Overview]
Overall, Port Henderson was transformed from a largely traditional, maritime-dependent 
fishing harbor into a pedestrianized, high-density residential and leisure destination. The 
most salient alterations include the total elimination of industrial fishing infrastructure, 
the substantial expansion of recreational facilities, and the enhancement of public 
transport links connecting the waterfront to inland districts.

[Paragraph 3: Body 1 — Coastal Waterfront & Marina Development]
Examining the shoreline, the commercial fish market and traditional docking piers that 
dominated the western coastline in 1995 were entirely demolished. In their place, a modern 
leisure yacht marina and a promenade flanked by luxury seafood restaurants and boutique 
cafes were erected. Meanwhile, the disused warehouse district situated immediately to the 
north of the harbor was repurposed into a multi-story apartment complex offering oceanfront 
housing.

[Paragraph 4: Body 2 — Inland & Infrastructure Reconfiguration]
Further inland, the rural green woodland located in the northeastern sector of the town was 
substantially cleared to accommodate a new golf course and luxury hotel resort. The main 
access road running north to south was upgraded into a dual-carriageway bypass, and a 
high-speed railway terminus was established directly adjacent to the commercial shopping 
center, which itself expanded westward across the former communal parkland. The historical 
residential quarter in the southeast remained virtually unaltered, preserving the town's 
original architectural character.
=========================================================================================

🔍 3. Band 9.0 Architectural Dissection

1. Task Achievement (Band 9.0):

- Identifies the underlying macro-shift (from maritime/industrial to leisure/residential) in the overview without missing any major map sectors.

2. Coherence & Cohesion (Band 9.0):

- Logically grouped by geographic focus: Paragraph 3 covers the waterfront/coastline; Paragraph 4 covers inland redevelopments and transit infrastructure.

3. Lexical Resource (Band 9.0):

- Natural, highly precise spatial vocabulary: salient alterations, pedestrianized, flanked by luxury restaurants, disused warehouse district, repurposed into, dual-carriageway bypass, railway terminus, communal parkland, virtually unaltered.

4. Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Band 9.0):

- Rich syntactic variety utilizing past simple passive, participial clauses ("offering oceanfront housing", "preserving the town's original character"), and relative positioning prepositions.
