🎧 IELTS Academic Listening: Section 1 Form Completion Masterclass

📋 Module Overview

  • Skill Focus: Section 1 (Everyday social context / 2 speakers)
  • Target Transition: Band 5.0 ➔ 6.0
  • Question Types: Form Completion, Table Completion, Note Completion (Max 2 words and/or a number)
  • Key Challenges: Correction traps (speaker changes mind), letters/numbers pronunciation, plural 's' endings, and spelling accuracy.

🎯 1. Core Examination Strategies for Band 6.0

1. Pre-Listening Word Limit Inspection:

- Always circle the instruction: NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER. Writing 3 words when the limit is 2 results in an immediate zero mark.

2. Predicting the Word Class and Information Type:

- Before the audio plays during the 30-second reading time, predict whether each blank requires a:

- Surname / Name (listen for spelling)

- Date / Day of the week

- Phone number / Reference code (letters + digits)

- Price / Currency symbol

- Noun (singular or plural)

3. The Classic "Correction" Distraction Pattern:

- In 90% of Section 1 recordings, the speaker will offer an initial answer, and then correct themselves:

- Speaker A: "My phone number is 07892 443 211."

- Speaker B: "Did you say 443?"

- Speaker A: "Oh sorry, no, that was my old one. It's actually 445 211."

- Never write down the first number without waiting for the speaker's confirmation.

🔑 4. Answer Key & Granular Distractor Analysis

1 Henstridge Spelled letter-by-letter (H-E-N-S-T-R-I-D-G-E). Spelling must be 100% exact.
2 07824 991 305 / 07824991305 Trap: Speaker initially gives 07798... (landline) before correcting to mobile 07824 991 305.
3 birthday (party) / 16th birthday Mentioned directly: "daughter's sixteenth birthday party".
4 21st / 21 Trap: Speaker initially mentions 14th, but family couldn't make it; settled on 21st.
5 45 Trap: 60 invitations sent out, but estimated attendance is 45.
6 7:30 / 7.30 / 7:30 PM Hall reserved from 2:00 PM to 7:30 PM.
7 hot beverages / hot drinks Trap: Cold snacks brought from outside, but kitchen needed for hot beverages.
8 projector Word limit: projector completes "projector and screen".
9 50 / 50.00 Basic hire is £180.00; refundable cleaning deposit is £50.00.
