# ✍️ IELTS Academic Writing Task 2: Discuss Both Views (Band 6.0 ➔ 7.0)

## 📋 Module Overview
- **Skill Focus**: Academic Writing Task 2 (Discussion + Personal View)
- **Target Transition**: Band 6.0 ➔ 7.0
- **The Core Problem for Band 6 Candidates**:
  - Writing 80% of the essay on their favored view and giving only 2 sentences to the opposing view (lacks parity/balance, capping Task Response at Band 6).
  - Failing to give their own opinion in the introduction and leaving it solely to the conclusion.

---

## 🎯 1. Master Structure for Balanced Discussion Essays

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            DISCUSS BOTH VIEWS ESSAY SKELETON                          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PARAGRAPH 1: INTRODUCTION (~50 words)                                                 |
| • Sentence 1: Background Paraphrase (Acknowledge that views diverge).                 |
| • Sentence 2: Thesis & Clear Opinion (State your stance while introducing both sides).|
|                                                                                       |
| PARAGRAPH 2: BODY 1 — EXAMINE VIEW A (The View You DON'T Agree With) (~95 words)       |
| • Topic Sentence: "On the one hand, proponents of [View A] argue that..."            |
| • Explanation: Explain the valid logic or societal benefit of this perspective.      |
| • Concrete Example: Support with an authentic real-world case.                       |
| • Concession / Limitation: Note why this approach is insufficient on its own.        |
|                                                                                       |
| PARAGRAPH 3: BODY 2 — EXAMINE VIEW B & YOUR VIEW (The View You SUPPORT) (~100 words)  |
| • Topic Sentence: "On the other hand, I advocate the view that [View B] is superior..."|
| • Explanation: Explain the comprehensive reasons supporting this stance.              |
| • Concrete Example: Statistical, economic, or environmental evidence.                 |
| • Substantive Synthesis: Highlight why View B delivers superior long-term outcomes.   |
|                                                                                       |
| PARAGRAPH 4: CONCLUSION (~40 words)                                                   |
| • Sentence 1: Restate that both perspectives have merits.                             |
| • Sentence 2: Reiterate your definitive preference clearly.                           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 📝 2. Practice Prompt & Full Band 7.5 Model Essay

### Prompt
> **Some people believe that international tourism brings significant economic benefits to host countries. Others argue that the environmental and cultural damage it causes far outweighs these advantages.**  
> *Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*  
> *Write at least 250 words.*

```
=========================================================================================
                                   BAND 7.5 MODEL ESSAY
=========================================================================================
[Paragraph 1: Introduction]
The rapid expansion of global travel in recent decades has ignited substantial debate 
regarding its overall societal contribution. While advocates emphasize that the tourism 
sector acts as a powerful catalyst for local economic development, critics contend that its 
ecological degradation and cultural erosion impose unacceptable costs. In my view, although 
the financial influx is undeniable, unregulated mass tourism causes environmental damage 
that necessitates strict sustainable management.

[Paragraph 2: Body 1 — The Economic Perspective]
On the one hand, proponents of international tourism highlight its indispensable role in 
economic growth and employment generation. In developing nations and remote coastal areas, 
foreign visitor spending directly stimulates hospitality, retail, and transportation 
industries, creating vital employment opportunities for local residents. Furthermore, tax 
revenues collected from tourism infrastructure allow municipal governments to fund public 
utilities, healthcare facilities, and transportation networks that would otherwise remain 
underdeveloped. For instance, in countries such as Thailand and the Maldives, tourism 
contributes over twenty percent of gross domestic product, serving as a primary driver of 
poverty alleviation.

[Paragraph 3: Body 2 — Ecological and Cultural Hazards & Author's Stance]
On the other hand, I side with those who argue that uncontrolled tourist expansion exerts 
devastating ecological and cultural pressures. The surge in international arrivals frequently 
leads to habitat destruction, severe coral reef bleaching from marine sports, and acute 
water shortages in fragile ecosystems. Moreover, the commercialization of sacred historical 
sites often dilutes indigenous traditions, reducing authentic heritage to superficial 
merchandise. A poignant example is Venice, where chronic overtourism has inflated residential 
living costs and caused severe architectural erosion, driving out native inhabitants. 
Without strict environmental quotas and sustainable regulations, short-term economic gains 
are rapidly eclipsed by permanent environmental destruction.

[Paragraph 4: Conclusion]
In conclusion, while international tourism generates substantial revenue and vital 
livelihoods for host communities, its unconstrained growth inflicts severe ecological and 
cultural harm. I firmly believe that governments must implement rigorous sustainable 
tourism policies to ensure environmental preservation takes precedence over unrestrained 
commercial profit.
=========================================================================================
```

## 🔍 3. Examiner Commentary & Criteria Alignment

1. **Task Response (Band 8.0)**:
   - Both perspectives are fully explored with substantive paragraphs and equal analytical weight.
   - The writer's opinion is explicit from paragraph 1 through paragraph 4.
2. **Coherence & Cohesion (Band 7.5)**:
   - Seamless progression between opposing viewpoints (*"On the one hand", "On the other hand, I side with those who", "In conclusion"*).
3. **Lexical Resource (Band 7.5)**:
   - High-scoring lexical density: *catalyst for local economic development, ecological degradation, cultural erosion, financial influx, poverty alleviation, commercialization of sacred sites, chronic overtourism, environmental quotas*.
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Band 7.5)**:
   - Effective use of complex subordinate clauses (*"While advocates emphasize... critics contend...", "tax revenues... allow governments to fund public utilities that would otherwise remain underdeveloped"*).
