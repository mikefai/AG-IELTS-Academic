# ✍️ IELTS Academic Writing Task 2: Two-Part Direct Questions (Band 9.0)

## 📋 Module Overview
- **Skill Focus**: Academic Writing Task 2 (Two-Part Direct Questions / Double Prompts)
- **Target Transition**: Band 8.5 ➔ 9.0
- **The Core Problem**: Candidates often answer one question thoroughly while neglecting the second question, or fail to establish an overarching philosophical connection between both inquiries.

---

## 🎯 1. Architectural Strategy for Two-Part Questions

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                              TWO-PART QUESTION BLUEPRINT                              |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PARAGRAPH 1: INTRODUCTION (~50 words)                                                 |
| • Sentence 1: Paraphrase the contextual background.                                   |
| • Sentence 2: Direct Thesis (Explicitly answer Question 1 AND Question 2).           |
|                                                                                       |
| PARAGRAPH 2: BODY 1 — ADDRESS QUESTION 1 FULLY (~100 words)                           |
| • Topic Sentence: Directly state your core answer to the first prompt question.      |
| • Elaboration & Conceptual Deepening: Explain underlying psychological/social factors.|
| • Real-world Academic Evidence: Concrete illustration.                                |
| • Concluding Synthesis: Conclude the answer to Question 1 cleanly.                    |
|                                                                                       |
| PARAGRAPH 3: BODY 2 — ADDRESS QUESTION 2 FULLY (~100 words)                           |
| • Topic Sentence: Directly transition to and answer the second prompt question.       |
| • Analytical Development: Explore implications, benefits, or systemic ramifications.  |
| • Empirical / Cultural Example: Substantiate with high-level illustration.            |
| • Concluding Synthesis: Bridge back to the core premise.                              |
|                                                                                       |
| PARAGRAPH 4: CONCLUSION (~40 words)                                                   |
| • Sentence 1: Reiterate your unified position answering both prompt questions.        |
| • Sentence 2: Broad concluding insight regarding future societal trajectory.          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 📝 2. Practice Prompt & Master Band 9.0 Model Response

### Prompt
> **In many cultures today, consumer spending on luxury material possessions has increased significantly. Why has this trend occurred? Is this a positive or negative development for society?**  
> *Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.*

```
=========================================================================================
                                    BAND 9.0 MODEL ESSAY
=========================================================================================
[Paragraph 1: Introduction]
In contemporary society, the acquisition of high-end commercial goods and luxury apparel has 
escalated dramatically across both developed and emerging economies. This phenomenon is 
fundamentally fueled by ubiquitous social media algorithms that amplify conspicuous 
consumption and socio-economic comparison. In my assessment, this trend represents an 
overwhelmingly deleterious development that exacerbates psychological distress and 
accelerates ecological depletion.

[Paragraph 2: Body 1 — Drivers of Conspicuous Consumption]
The proliferation of luxury consumerism is predominantly catalyzed by the pervasive reach of 
digital media platforms and targeted influencer marketing. In previous eras, individuals 
measured their standard of living relative to immediate geographic peers; today, however, 
platforms such as Instagram and TikTok expose billions of users to curated, idealized 
lifestyles of immense wealth. Consequently, possessing prestigious designer items has become 
a psychological proxy for personal worth, social status, and professional validation. 
Furthermore, the widespread availability of consumer credit and "buy-now-pay-later" schemes 
has democratized access to non-essential luxury items, enabling younger demographics to 
incur substantial debt in pursuit of ephemeral social prestige.

[Paragraph 3: Body 2 — Societal & Ecological Consequences]
This relentless prioritization of material acquisition exerts profoundly detrimental 
effects on societal well-being and environmental sustainability. Psychologically, 
conspicuous consumption reinforces a "hedonic treadmill" in which the transient satisfaction 
derived from purchasing expensive items rapidly dissipates, fostering chronic anxiety, 
inadequacy, and clinical depression. On a macroeconomic and environmental level, manufacturing 
fast-turnaround luxury goods—such as designer apparel, high-performance electronics, and 
luxury vehicles—accelerates resource extraction, intensive carbon emissions, and non-recyclable 
waste generation. When societal status is indexed to disposable luxury rather than civic 
contribution or intellectual enrichment, collective community cohesion inevitably deteriorates.

[Paragraph 4: Conclusion]
In conclusion, the surge in luxury spending is driven by algorithmic social media comparison 
and accessible consumer credit. This development is distinctly negative, fostering widespread 
psychological dissatisfaction while imposing unsustainable ecological strains on our planet.
=========================================================================================
```

## 🔍 3. Band 9.0 Structural & Lexical Analysis

1. **Task Response (Band 9.0)**:
   - Paragraph 2 answers *Why has this occurred?* (Social media exposure, status proxy, credit access).
   - Paragraph 3 answers *Is this positive or negative?* (Clearly argues it is negative on psychological, ecological, and societal grounds).
   - Unambiguous position maintained throughout.
2. **Coherence & Cohesion (Band 9.0)**:
   - Effortless cohesion: Paragraph topic sentences directly answer each prompt prompt prompt, utilizing organic lexical cohesion rather than mechanical formulaic linkers.
3. **Lexical Resource (Band 9.0)**:
   - Native-level academic precision: *conspicuous consumption, socio-economic comparison, deleterious development, ubiquitous algorithms, psychological proxy, democratized access, ephemeral social prestige, hedonic treadmill, transient satisfaction, community cohesion*.
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Band 9.0)**:
   - Full flexibility across complex clauses, inverted conditional structures, and participial constructions without a single error.
