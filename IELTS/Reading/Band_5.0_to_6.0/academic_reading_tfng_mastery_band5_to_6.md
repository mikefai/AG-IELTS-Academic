# 📖 IELTS Academic Reading: True / False / Not Given (T/F/NG) Masterclass

## 📋 Module Overview
- **Target Transition**: Band 5.0 ➔ 6.0
- **Core Question Type**: True / False / Not Given & Yes / No / Not Given
- **The Core Problem for Band 5 Students**: Confusing `FALSE` with `NOT GIVEN`, making assumptions based on outside real-world knowledge, and missing qualifying adverbs (*always, sometimes, exclusively, occasionally*).

---

## 🎯 1. The Definitive T/F/NG Logic Framework

```
+----------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| CLASSIFICATION | PRECISE EXAM DEFINITION                                               |
+----------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TRUE           | The statement AGREEES WITH the information in the passage.           |
|                | (Every factual element matches or is directly paraphrased).           |
+----------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| FALSE          | The statement CONTRADICTS / OPPOSES the information in the passage.  |
|                | (The text states the exact opposite or an incompatible fact).         |
+----------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| NOT GIVEN      | The statement CANNOT BE CONFIRMED or DISPROVED by the passage.        |
|                | (There is no information, or the fact is partially missing).         |
+----------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### The 3 Golden Rules
1. **Never Assume from Real-World Knowledge**: Even if a statement is factually true in real life (e.g. *"Water boils at 100 degrees"*), if the passage does not mention it, the answer is `NOT GIVEN`.
2. **Watch the Modifiers & Quantifiers**:
   - `All` vs `Some` vs `The majority`
   - `Always` vs `Often` vs `Under specific circumstances`
   - `Proved` vs `Suggested` vs `Hypothesized`
3. **Sequential Order**: T/F/NG questions almost always follow the chronological order of the text. If you find Question 1 in Paragraph 2 and Question 3 in Paragraph 4, Question 2 MUST be between them.

## 📄 2. Academic Reading Passage: The Rise of Vertical Farming

```
[Paragraph A]
With the global human population projected to surpass 9.7 billion by 2050, traditional agriculture faces insurmountable land and water constraints. Modern agricultural scientists argue that horizontal soil farming is approaching its ecological limits, having already degraded over a third of the planet's arable land through intensive chemical fertilization and topsoil erosion. In response, an innovative agricultural paradigm—vertical farming—has emerged as a viable solution to urban food insecurity.

[Paragraph B]
Vertical farming involves cultivating crops in vertically stacked layers inside fully controlled indoor environments. Rather than relying on natural sunlight and fertile topsoil, these high-tech indoor facilities utilize artificial light-emitting diode (LED) arrays calibrated to specific photosynthetic wavelengths, alongside advanced hydroponic or aeroponic nutrient delivery systems. In aeroponics, plant roots are suspended in air and misted at precise intervals with a mineral-rich liquid solution, which reduces total water consumption by up to 95 percent compared to conventional open-field cultivation.

[Paragraph C]
Furthermore, because vertical farms are completely isolated from external environmental fluctuations, they remain impervious to unpredictable weather patterns such as severe droughts, unseasonal frosts, and torrential floods. Moreover, enclosed indoor facilities eliminate the need for chemical pesticides and herbicides, as crop pests cannot infiltrate the sealed biosecure growing chambers. Consequently, produce can be harvested year-round on continuous 30-day crop cycles, yielding up to ten times more leafy greens per square meter than conventional outdoor farming.

[Paragraph D]
Despite these compelling ecological benefits, vertical farming currently encounters severe economic headwinds. The initial capital expenditure required to install specialized climate control systems, robotic harvesting units, and high-efficiency LED lights is exceptionally high. Additionally, the continuous electrical energy required to power illumination and air filtration systems remains a significant operational cost, particularly in regions where electricity grids rely predominantly on expensive fossil fuels rather than cheap renewable sources.
```

## ❓ 3. Practice Items: True / False / Not Given

*Do the following statements agree with the information given in the Reading Passage?*  
*In boxes 1–6 on your answer sheet, write:*
- **TRUE** if the statement agrees with the information
- **FALSE** if the statement contradicts the information
- **NOT GIVEN** if there is no information on this

**1.** Conventional horizontal farming has caused degradation to more than one-third of the world's fertile land.  
**2.** Aeroponic systems submerge plant root systems underwater continuously to accelerate growth.  
**3.** Vertical farming facilities produce less carbon emissions than traditional farms.  
**4.** Enclosed vertical farming systems require minimal chemical pesticides to control insect populations.  
**5.** Produce from vertical farms can be harvested continuously without being limited by natural weather seasons.  
**6.** High electricity costs are currently a major financial challenge for vertical farming operations.

## 🔑 4. Answer Key & Step-by-Step Rationales

| Q | Answer | Textual Evidence & Logical Breakdown |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **TRUE** | *Passage [Para A]*: "...having already degraded **over a third** of the planet's arable land..." <br>*Analysis*: "More than one-third" perfectly matches "over a third". Factually identical. |
| **2** | **FALSE** | *Passage [Para B]*: "In aeroponics, plant roots are **suspended in air and misted** at precise intervals..." <br>*Analysis*: Submerging roots underwater is the opposite of suspending roots in air and misting them. Contradicts text. |
| **3** | **NOT GIVEN** | *Passage [Para B/D]*: The text discusses water savings (95%) and electricity consumption, but **nowhere** does it compare total carbon emissions between vertical and traditional farms. Do not guess! |
| **4** | **FALSE** | *Passage [Para C]*: "...enclosed indoor facilities **eliminate the need** for chemical pesticides and herbicides..." <br>*Analysis*: "Require minimal" contradicts "eliminate the need" (which means zero pesticides needed). |
| **5** | **TRUE** | *Passage [Para C]*: "...produce can be harvested **year-round on continuous 30-day crop cycles**... impervious to unpredictable weather patterns..." <br>*Analysis*: Matches "harvested continuously without being limited by weather seasons". |
| **6** | **TRUE** | *Passage [Para D]*: "...the continuous electrical energy required... remains a **significant operational cost**..." <br>*Analysis*: Matches "major financial challenge". |
