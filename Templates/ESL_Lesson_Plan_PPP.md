- **Handout**: Printable gap-fill and prompt cards.

## ⏱️ Detailed Stage Breakdown (PPP Framework)

### 1. Warmer / Lead-In (5–8 mins)
- **Stage Aim**: Activate schemata, set the context, and stimulate interest.
- **Teacher Script / Activity**:
  - Show a dramatic image or ask a thought-provoking open question: *"Have you ever...?"*
  - Pair-share for 2 minutes; elicit 2–3 responses to the whole class.

### 2. Presentation & Clarification (15 mins)
- **Stage Aim**: Clarify Meaning, Form, and Pronunciation (MFP).
- **Contextual Marker**: Write a model sentence on the board extracted from the lead-in.
- **Concept Check Questions (CCQs)**:
  - *CCQ 1*: Did this event happen in the past? *(Yes)*
  - *CCQ 2*: Do we know the exact time? *(No)*
  - *CCQ 3*: Is it connected to the present? *(Yes)*
- **Form & Phonology**: Highlight contracted forms on the board and drill chorally/individually.

### 3. Controlled Practice (15 mins)
- **Stage Aim**: Restrict language use to focus entirely on grammatical accuracy.
- **Activity**: Individual gap-fill / sentence transformation worksheet (8–10 items).
- **Feedback**: Peer-check in pairs, followed by whole-class confirmation and whiteboard validation.

### 4. Freer Production / Communicative Task (17 mins)
- **Stage Aim**: Develop fluency by applying the target structure in an authentic scenario.
- **Activity**: Role-play / Find Someone Who / Information Gap / Dilemma Debate.
- **Teacher Role**: Monitor discreetly, taking notes on errors for delayed feedback (do not interrupt fluency).

### 5. Wrap-Up & Delayed Error Correction (5 mins)
- **Stage Aim**: Highlight common structural errors observed during freer production.
- **Activity**: Write 3–4 incorrect sentences heard during monitoring on the board and elicit peer corrections.
