## 🔍 Four-Criterion Band Descriptor Analysis

### 1. Task Response (TR)
- **Position**: Fully clear and consistent throughout the entire essay.
- **Idea Development**: Each main idea is extended with logical causal links and grounded with realistic illustrative context.

### 2. Coherence & Cohesion (CC)
- **Macro-Structure**: Clear 4-paragraph organizational layout.
- **Micro-Cohesion**: Skilful use of referential pronouns, thematic paragraph transitions, and discourse markers (*Notwithstanding this perspective, Consequently, On the other hand*).

### 3. Lexical Resource (LR)
- **Topic Lexicon**: Employs sophisticated collocation chains and context-specific terminology with natural precision.
- **Idiomatic/Rare Usage**: Natural control of stylistic nuance without forced thesaurus words.

### 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)
- **Sentence Architecture**: Blends simple sentences for rhetorical emphasis with complex structures (cleft sentences, inverted conditionals, non-finite clauses).
- **Error Rate**: 100% error-free syntax and punctuation.
