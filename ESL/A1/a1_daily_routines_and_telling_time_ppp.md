# 🌍 ESL A1 Lesson Plan: Daily Routines & Telling Time

## 🎯 Lesson Information & Objectives
- **Target Level**: CEFR A1 (Breakthrough / High Beginner)
- **Lesson Duration**: 60 Minutes (Scaffolded PPP Framework)
- **Target Language (MFP)**:
  - **Meaning**: Expressing habitual daily actions, morning/evening schedules, and exact times of day.
  - **Form**:
    - Affirmative: `Subject + Verb(s/es) + at [Time]` (*e.g., "I wake up at 7:00 AM.", "He wakes up at 7:30 AM."*)
    - Negative: `Subject + do/does not + Base Verb` (*e.g., "She doesn't drink coffee."*)
    - Question: `Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb?` (*e.g., "What time do you have breakfast?"*)
  - **Pronunciation**:
    - Final *-s/-es* endings: $/s/$ (*e.g., sleeps*), $/z/$ (*e.g., cleans, plays*), $/ɪz/$ (*e.g., watches, washes*).
    - Weak form of *at*: $/ət/$.
- **Key Vocabulary**: *wake up, brush teeth, take a shower, have breakfast, catch the bus, start work/school, cook dinner, go to bed; o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to*.

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Lesson Execution Plan

### Stage 1: Warm-Up & Lead-In (7 mins)
- **Goal**: Engage learners and activate existing time/action schemata.
- **Activity**: Show an analog clock face showing 07:00 AM and a picture of a steaming cup of coffee.
- **Teacher Script**: *"Good morning class! Look at this clock. What time is it? (7 o'clock!). What is the very first thing you do when you wake up?"* Elicit 3–4 gestures and words from students onto the board.

---

### Stage 2: Presentation & Clarification (15 mins)
- **Context**: Introduce "Alex's Busy Monday" via a visual timeline flashcard sequence.
- **Target Model Sentences**:
  1. *Alex wakes up at half past six.*
  2. *He catches the bus at seven o'clock.*
  3. *He doesn't eat breakfast at home.*
- **Concept Check Questions (CCQs)**:
  - *CCQ 1*: Is Alex doing this right now at this exact moment? *(No, it is his daily habit).*
  - *CCQ 2*: Does he do this only on Monday or every weekday? *(Every weekday / regular routine).*
  - *CCQ 3*: Why do we say "Alex wake**s** up" but "I wake up"? *(Because Alex is he/she/it $\rightarrow$ add -s).*
- **Phonology Drill**:
  - Drill the 3 endings in rhythm:
    - $/s/$: *eats, cooks, sleeps*
    - $/z/$: *reads, leaves, cleans*
    - $/ɪz/$: *watches, washes, relaxes*

---

### Stage 3: Controlled Practice Worksheet (15 mins)

#### Exercise A: Verb Form Transformation
*Complete the sentences with the correct form of the verb in parentheses:*

1. Maria ____________ (wake up) at 6:30 AM every morning.
2. They ____________ (not / drink) tea in the morning; they prefer orange juice.
3. David ____________ (catch) the subway to work at quarter past eight.
4. What time ____________ you ____________ (have) lunch on Saturdays?
5. My sister ____________ (watch) the evening news before dinner.

#### Exercise B: Time Matching
*Match the digital time with the correct English time expression:*

- **1.** 08:15 ➔ [ A) half past eight | B) quarter past eight | C) quarter to eight ]
- **2.** 09:30 ➔ [ A) half past nine | B) nine o'clock | C) half to nine ]
- **3.** 06:45 ➔ [ A) quarter past six | B) quarter to seven | C) half past six ]

---

### Stage 4: Freer Production / Speaking Lab (18 mins)

#### Pair Activity: "The Daily Life Survey"
Students work in pairs. Each student asks their partner 4 routine questions and records the answers to report back to the class:

```text
Student A: "What time do you usually wake up?"
Student B: "I wake up at seven o'clock. What about you?"
Student A: "I wake up at half past six because I catch the early bus."
```

**Pair Speaking Prompts**:
1. *What time do you have breakfast, and what do you eat?*
2. *How do you travel to school or work?*
3. *What do you do to relax in the evening?*
4. *What time do you go to sleep on weekends?*

---

### Stage 5: Wrap-Up & Delayed Error Feedback (5 mins)
- Write 3 anonymized student errors heard during monitoring on the whiteboard:
  - *❌ "He wake up at 7."* ➔ **Correction**: *"He wake**s** up at 7."*
  - *❌ "She don't catch the bus."* ➔ **Correction**: *"She **doesn't** catch the bus."*
  - *❌ "At half past eight o'clock."* ➔ **Correction**: *"At half past eight." (Do not combine half past with o'clock).*

---

## 🔑 Answer Key & Teacher Notes

### Exercise A Answers:
1. **wakes up** *(3rd person singular -s)*
2. **do not drink / don't drink** *(plural subject 'they')*
3. **catches** *(verb ending in -ch takes -es, pronounced /ɪz/)*
4. **do you have** *(Present Simple question structure)*
5. **watches** *(3rd person singular -es, pronounced /ɪz/)*

### Exercise B Answers:
1. **B) quarter past eight**
2. **A) half past nine**
3. **B) quarter to seven** (6:45 is 15 minutes before 7:00)
