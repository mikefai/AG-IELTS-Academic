## 📘 2. Core Grammar & Meaning Systems

### A. Module A: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

#### 1. Meaning & Concept
- **Past Simple**: The time period is **finished** and closed in the past.
  - *Example*: *"I bought a new laptop last week."* (Last week is finished).
- **Present Perfect**: The action is **connected to the present moment** in 3 primary ways:
  1. **Life Experience** (time unspecified): *"Have you ever been to Japan?"*
  2. **Recent Result** (action happened, result is now): *"I have lost my keys (I can't open the door now)."*
  3. **Unfinished State / Time Period**: *"I've lived here for 3 years (I still live here)."* / *"I've had two coffees today (today is not over)."*

#### 2. Spoken Forms & Contractions
- `I have` $\rightarrow$ **/aɪv/** (`I've`)
- `He has` $\rightarrow$ **/hiːz/** (`He's`)
- `Have not` $\rightarrow$ **/ˈhæv.ənt/** (`haven't`)
- `Has not` $\rightarrow$ **/ˈhæz.ənt/** (`hasn't`)

#### 3. Signal Word Bank
- **Past Simple**: `yesterday`, `... ago`, `last night/week/year`, `in 2021`, `when I was younger`.
- **Present Perfect**: `ever`, `never`, `just`, `already`, `yet`, `since`, `for`, `this morning/week` (unfinished).

---

### B. Module B: Past Simple vs Past Perfect (Storytelling)

#### 1. Meaning & The "Earlier Past"
We use Past Perfect (`had + V3`) when narrating in the past and jumping back to an action that happened **before** the main past storyline.
- *Event 1 (First in time)*: The bus had left (`had + V3`).
- *Event 2 (Second in time)*: We arrived at the station (Past Simple).
- *Combined*: *"When we arrived at the station, the bus had already left."* (We missed the bus!).

#### 2. Spoken Contractions
- `I had` $\rightarrow$ **/aɪd/** (`I'd`)
- `She had` $\rightarrow$ **/ʃiːd/** (`She'd`)

#### 3. High-Frequency Storytelling Frames
1. `By the time I arrived, everyone had gone home.`
2. `When I got to the airport, I realised I had left my passport at home.`
3. `After they had finished dinner, they went for a walk.`

## 🇹🇷 3. Turkish Learner L1 Notes (Neden Karıştırılır?)

1. **Eksik Zaman Kavramı**: Türkçe'de "Present Perfect" yapısının bağımsız bir zaman kipi yoktur. Hem *"Dün sinemaya gittim"* hem de *"Daha önce hiç Londra'da bulundun mu?"* cümlelerinde *-di'li* geçmiş zaman kullanılır.
2. **-den beri / for Hatası**: Türkçe konuşurları *"3 yıldır burada yaşıyorum"* derken geniş/şimdiki zaman kullandığı için İngilizce'de *"I live here for 3 years"* hatasını yaparlar. İngilizce kural: Eylem geçmişte başlayıp şimdiye kadar geliyorsa **Present Perfect** (`I have lived here for 3 years`) zorunludur.
3. **Zaman Zarflarına Dikkat**: Cümlede net bir geçmiş zaman zarfı (`yesterday`, `ago`, `last year`, `in 2019`) varsa **asla** Present Perfect kullanılmaz; **Past Simple (V2)** kullanılır.

## 🗣️ 4. Classroom Speaking Tasks

### Task 1: Warmer (Pair Questions & Model Answers)
1. **Q**: *"Have you ever lost something valuable while traveling?"*  
   **Follow-Up**: *"When did it happen? What did you do?"*  
   **Model Answer**: *"Yes, I have! I lost my wallet in Rome two years ago. I went to the police station."*
2. **Q**: *"Have you ever tried an extreme sport (e.g., rafting, skiing)?"*  
   **Follow-Up**: *"Where were you? How did you feel?"*  
   **Model Answer**: *"Yes, I've tried snowboarding. I went to Uludağ last winter. It was exciting but scary!"*
3. **Q**: *"Have you ever met a famous person?"*  
   **Follow-Up**: *"Who was it? What did you say?"*  
   **Model Answer**: *"No, I've never met a celebrity, but my brother saw a famous singer at the airport in 2023."*
4. **Q**: *"Have you ever missed a train, flight, or bus?"*  
   **Follow-Up**: *"Why were you late? What happened next?"*  
   **Model Answer**: *"Yes, I missed my bus last week because I had overslept. I had to take a taxi."*

### Task 2: 60-Second Monologue Speaking Cards
- **Card 1 (Story with a Twist)**: Talk about a time you went somewhere and something had already happened before you arrived.
  - *Scaffold*: 1. Start with *"A memorable day was when I went to..."* $\rightarrow$ 2. Add detail: *"When I arrived, I realised that [someone] had already..."* $\rightarrow$ 3. Say how you felt: *"I was surprised/relieved because..."*
- **Card 2 (Unforgettable Experience)**: Talk about an exciting experience you have had in your life.
  - *Scaffold*: 1. Start with *"One of the most exciting things I have ever done is..."* $\rightarrow$ 2. Give details: *"It happened two years ago when I visited..."* $\rightarrow$ 3. Say what you learned: *"Since that day, I have always loved..."*
- **Card 3 (Weekly Accomplishments)**: Talk about things you have done so far this week and what happened yesterday.
  - *Scaffold*: 1. Start with *"So far this week, I've been quite busy with..."* $\rightarrow$ 2. Specific past event: *"Yesterday, I finished/met/went to..."* $\rightarrow$ 3. Contrast: *"I haven't completed my project yet, but I've already..."*

| **2** | **A** (`bought`) | Past Simple vs. Present Perfect | Specific finished past time (`last Saturday`). |
| **3** | **C** (`had left`) | Past Simple vs. Past Perfect | Earlier past action before arriving at airport. |

### Detailed Distractor Rationales

- **Item 1**: *(A) went* is incorrect because 'went' requires a specific past time frame; *(C) had been* is incorrect because there is no secondary later past reference point; *(D) was going* is an ongoing continuous action, not cumulative life count.
- **Item 2**: *(B) has bought* incorrectly pairs Present Perfect with a finished time marker (`last Saturday`); *(C) had bought* has no subsequent past reference; *(D) buys* is habitual present.
- **Item 3**: *(A) left* fails to signal the sequence of events; *(B) have left* introduces present tense into a past narrative; *(D) were leaving* suggests an interrupted ongoing action.
- **Item 4**: *(A) Did / eat* sounds unnatural for general life experiences without a specific time; *(C) Had / eaten* is unnecessary past perfect; *(D) Do / eat* asks about routine habits.
- **Item 5**: *(A) lost* does not emphasize the immediate consequence; *(C) had lost* requires a past narrative perspective; *(D) was losing* implies an action in progress.
- **Item 14**: *(B) has written* is a classic error; living authors take Present Perfect (*"Stephen King has written many books"*), but deceased historical figures take Past Simple (*"Shakespeare wrote"*).
- **Item 20**: *(B) have called* is a common Turkish interference error; English strictly requires Past Simple with *ago*.
