## 🗂️ Target Seed Vocabulary Bank (Recycled 80%+)

### 1. Jobs & Careers
- `career`: A job or profession you do for a long period of your life.
- `salary`: The regular monthly earnings you receive for work.
- `apply for`: To formally request a job position.
- `skills`: Learned abilities developed through practice.
- `experience`: Practical knowledge gained from past work.
- `boss`: The supervisor in charge of workplace operations.
- `colleague`: A coworker in the same team or organization.
- `interview`: A formal evaluation meeting for job candidates.
- `qualifications`: Official certificates and degrees verifying capability.
- `dream job`: The ideal profession someone desires most.
- `earn`: To obtain money in exchange for labor.
- `hire`: To employ someone for wages.
- `resign`: To officially leave an employment position.
- `promote`: To advance an employee to a higher rank.
- `freelance`: Working on individual contracts rather than for one employer.
- `entrepreneur`: An individual who launches a commercial enterprise.
- `remote working`: Performing job duties from home using digital tools.

### 2. School & Academic Pressures
- `grades`: Scores or letters representing academic performance.
- `exam`: A formal test evaluating subject mastery.
- `deadline`: The final agreed date/time for work submission.
- `stressed`: Experiencing emotional strain and anxiety.
- `pressure`: Demands from family, teachers, or peers.
- `revise`: Reviewing course materials prior to evaluation.
- `homework`: Assignments allocated for completion after school.
- `expectations`: Standards anticipated by parents or educators.
- `compare`: Measuring one's achievements against peers.
- `compete`: Striving to outperform others.
- `fail`: Not meeting the minimum passing benchmark.
- `pass`: Successfully reaching or exceeding the exam benchmark.
- `scholarship`: Educational funding granted on academic/sporting merit.
- `workload`: The aggregate quantity of study or tasks required.
- `burnout`: Physical and cognitive collapse due to chronic overload.
- `balance`: Equilibrium between academic duties and rest.
- `mental health`: Psychological, emotional, and social well-being.

### 3. Feelings & Opinion Adjectives
- `ambitious`, `creative`, `challenging`, `rewarding`, `exhausted`, `motivated`, `overwhelmed`, `proud`, `confident`, `disappointed`.

## 📐 Compact Grammar Notes (5 Essential Forms)

1. **`want to` / `would like to` + Verb (Dream Jobs)**
   - *Form:* `Subject + want(s) to / would like to + base verb`
   - *Example:* "She would like to work remotely as a freelance graphic designer."
   - *Teen Mistake:* `*I would like to working in a game studio.*` ➔ `✅ I would like to work in a game studio.`
2. **Second Conditional for Unreal Situations**
   - *Form:* `If + past simple, ... would/could + base verb`
   - *Example:* "If I could choose any career, I would be a marine biologist."
   - *Teen Mistake:* `*If I will have time, I would revise.*` ➔ `✅ If I had time, I would revise.`
3. **Modals of Obligation & Advice (`have to`, `must`, `should`)**
   - *Form:* `must/have to + base verb` (obligation), `should + base verb` (advice), `don't have to` (optional).
   - *Example:* "You don't have to get 100% on every test to have a successful life."
   - *Teen Mistake:* `*You must to revise tonight.*` ➔ `✅ You must revise tonight.`
4. **Quantifiers: `too + noun` vs `(not) enough`**
   - *Form:* `too much + uncountable` (homework), `too many + countable` (exams), `(not) enough + noun`.
   - *Example:* "I have too much homework and not enough sleep this week."
   - *Teen Mistake:* `*Our teacher gave us too many homework.*` ➔ `✅ ...too much homework.`
5. **Comparatives & Superlatives**
   - *Form:* `adj + -er than`, `more + adj + than`, `the most + adj`, `better / worse`.
   - *Example:* "Physics is more challenging than history, but surgeon is the most stressful job."
   - *Teen Mistake:* `*Being a boss is more better than an employee.*` ➔ `✅ ...much better...`
